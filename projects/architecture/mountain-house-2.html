Residential Architecture

Hill House

A 3-bedroom, climate-responsive mountain residence in Murree, Pakistan, spanning a 33' x 39' (10.06m x 11.89m) footprint. Key programs are oriented toward the valley to maximize panoramic views and visual connectivity. The architecture is engineered with durable, weather-resistant assemblies to withstand heavy regional snowfall and seasonal freeze-thaw cycles.
